Otagi Nenbutsu-ji Temple

Sagano, Western Kyoto · 400 Moss Statues & Hand-Carved Rakan

Field Journal & Impression

Perched at the quiet terminus of the traditional preservation district, this secluded hillside temple offers a sensory antidote to the crowded avenues below. Over one thousand whimsical stone sculptures—each uniquely expressive and veiled in lush mountain moss—rest beneath ancient cedar and Japanese maples. The subtle scent of damp river stone and burning cypress incense anchors a sanctuary best witnessed in soft morning light.
